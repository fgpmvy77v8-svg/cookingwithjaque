Oi, I'm Jackie

Brazilian by training, but I'll cook whatever your family loves.

I'm Jackie Wheeler. I trained in Brazil and hold a Bachelor of Science in Food and Nutrition from Universidade Anhembi Morumbi in São Paulo, with coursework in food quality management and hygiene control.

I grew up on feijão, moqueca and pão de queijo, and I cook all of it. But most weeks I'm making Mediterranean plates, roasted chicken and vegetables, pasta the kids will actually eat, and dessert for Friday. Entrées to desserts, whatever the household is in the mood for.

Today I cook weekly for private families across Los Angeles and Orange County — including professional athletes and public figures — and I work dinners, parties and events when a household is entertaining.

  • Menus tailored to dietary needs, allergies and preferences
  • Everything portioned into individual containers and labeled
  • I track your fridge and pantry so less food gets thrown out
  • Currently completing additional coursework in vegan cuisine

Weekly meal prep

One session in your kitchen covers the week. Here's exactly what happens:

  1. I suggest a balanced weekly menu for your household
  2. I shop that morning for fresh, carefully selected ingredients
  3. I cook the week's meals in your kitchen
  4. Everything is portioned into individual containers and labeled
  5. I organize it all in the fridge so it's obvious what to grab
  6. I clean the kitchen before I leave
