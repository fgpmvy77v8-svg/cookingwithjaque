Oi, I'm Jackie

A cook from São Paulo who studied the nutrition side too.

I'm Jaqueline — everyone calls me Jackie. I trained in Brazil and hold a Bachelor of Science in Food and Nutrition from Universidade Anhembi Morumbi in São Paulo, along with coursework in food quality management and hygiene control.

That's why I cook the way I do: seasonal, organic where it counts, built around what each household needs rather than one menu for everybody. Right now I cook weekly for private families across Los Angeles and Orange County, and I work dinners, parties and events alongside other chefs when a household is entertaining.

  • Menus tailored to dietary needs, allergies and preferences
  • Everything portioned into individual containers and labeled
  • I track what's in your fridge and pantry so less food gets thrown out
  • Currently completing additional coursework in vegan cuisine

Weekly meal prep

One session in your kitchen covers the week. Here's exactly what happens:

  1. I suggest a balanced weekly menu for your household
  2. I shop that morning for fresh, carefully selected ingredients
  3. I cook the week's meals in your kitchen
  4. Everything is portioned into individual containers and labeled
  5. I organize it all in the fridge so it's obvious what to grab
  6. I clean the kitchen before I leave
