Oi, I'm Jaque

I learned to cook in my grandmother's kitchen. I learned nutrition because my clients asked.

I grew up cooking in São Paulo, where a good meal is measured in how long people stay at the table. When I moved to Los Angeles, my clients were athletes, new parents, and people juggling long days — they wanted that same food, but they also wanted to know exactly what was in it.

So I learned to do both. Every plate I send out is labeled: calories, protein, carbs, fat. Nothing is stripped of flavor to hit a number — I'd rather adjust the portion than the seasoning.

  • Certified in food safety (ServSafe) & sports nutrition fundamentals
  • Cook in your kitchen or prep and deliver, your call
  • Fluent in Portuguese, English, and "no dairy, please"

How it works

From first call to food in your fridge

  1. 01

    Tell me how you eat

    A 20-minute call: your goals, allergies, what you're tired of, who else is at the table.

  2. 02

    I build the menu

    You get a proposed week with macros per dish. Swap anything before I shop.

  3. 03

    Cook day

    I shop that morning and cook in your kitchen or mine. Everything is labeled and dated.

  4. 04

    We adjust

    Portions too big, protein too low, craving more feijão? Tell me and next week shifts.
